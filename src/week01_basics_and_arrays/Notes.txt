WEEK 1 LEARNING NOTES:
1. Two-Pointer Swap Technique: Used in Reversearray and Rotationarray
to modify values in-place with O(1) space complexity.
2. Read/Write Placement Pointer: Used in MoveZeroes to filter
 out numbers sequentially without creating a new array.
3. Single-Pass Scanning: Used in Minandmax to find upper
 and lower bounds simultaneously in a single loop, cutting operations in half.
